FlightGear Boeing 777 exterior models and adapted liveries
========================================================

The 777-200LR passenger and 777F cargo models are adapted from community
FlightGear aircraft. They are illustrative models, not manufacturer CAD or
certified flight-training systems. The simulator retains its simplified 747
cockpit and flight dynamics.

Passenger model (777-200LR):
  Source: https://github.com/franck-vmd/Boeing-777-Flightgear-2048
  Revision: b64215060a0591ee39b9ecd7558db274014be223
  Source mesh: 777-VMD/Models/Liveries-200LR/777-200LR.ac
  Source animation: 777-VMD/Models/777-200LR-v2.xml
  Converted asset: 777-200LR-2048.candidate.glb

Cargo model (777-200F / 777F):
  Source: https://github.com/franck-vmd/Boeing-777-Flightgear
  Revision: cfa61378d1210a33e0520d4459d0baf1cf1511f5

manufacturer names or artwork may be separate trademarks; their inclusion does
not imply endorsement or grant trademark rights.

Source meshes, associated textures and XML are included in the GitHub
repository; bulky conversion inputs are excluded from the runtime image.
Conversion and texture-resizing scripts are under tools/
(convert-b777.mjs, convert-b777-triangulation.mjs, convert-b777-variants.json
and convert-b777-textures.py). The converted textures are reduced to at most
1024 pixels. The 777F passenger-window opening is covered by a small
UV-mapped patch; it is not a certified or topologically welded conversion.
Animation bindings, landing-gear contact and controls are approximations.

The 12 passenger atlases and the two freight atlases in assets/b777-liveries/
are unofficial adaptations on GPL FlightGear texture/UV sources. Their artwork
uses project-created text and vector elements, not copied aircraft photographs.
The passenger atlas generator also uses two 200LR texture source files from the
first repository at revision cfa61378d1210a33e0520d4459d0baf1cf1511f5.
FedEx styling is based on publicly visible 777F aircraft; the SF 777F styling
is an illustrative adaptation of SF 747 freighter references, not a claim
that an SF 777F is in service. No combination guarantees the registration,
engine option or paint details of a particular real aircraft.

A small texture override from the passenger source is unavailable upstream;
the converted aircraft retains the original mesh texture instead. Some source
polygons required approximate triangulation. Neither detail is represented as
manufacturer-verified geometry.
