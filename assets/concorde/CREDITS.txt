Licensing evidence and limitation
---------------------------------
FlightGear's project license page says FlightGear is GPL:
https://www.flightgear.org/about/license/
Its official repository policy says aircraft released under GPL2+ may be
hosted on FGAddon and requires FGAddon contributions to be GPL compatible:
https://www.flightgear.org/about/policy/
The FlightGear data repository README says the project is GPL'd and refers
to COPYING: https://sourceforge.net/p/flightgear/fgdata/ci/next/tree/README
A Concorde fork containing the same four blobs carries GPL version 2 text:
https://github.com/FGMEMBERS/Concorde/blob/master/COPYING
That COPYING was added by the fork maintainer in 2015:
https://github.com/FGMEMBERS/Concorde/commit/70a835845bdea09115e2541a3c68b64cdec62743
The present four source blobs match that fork's master (Git blob IDs:

explicit individual license grant for Bogey's mesh or Hoffmann's livery.
The 2015 fork license is a repository-level assertion by a fork maintainer,
not a direct statement from either artist. Thus GPLv2 is the documented
project/fork license basis, but independent rights-holder permission for
these particular assets could not be verified. Obtain upstream/rights-holder
confirmation before relying on this provenance for redistribution. The
actual GPLv2 text from that fork is included as COPYING. Names and airline
marks can also have separate trademark restrictions.

Adaptation and limits
---------------------
The adapter uses the AC source in metres, rotating the native frame to the
simulator's +X-forward convention. It selects only BA and AF airline paints;
the upstream XML's airframe/nose paint targets and approximate hinge axes
inform the implementation. It drives droop nose, visor, main/nose/tail gear,
selected gear doors, wheels, elevons, rudder and four pairs of nozzle/reverse
buckets. Bucket axes and forward/reverse angles come from the upstream model
XML and engine-system definitions; the simulator's reverse transition timing
is simplified. The gear linkage, visor sequencing and control mixing are also
simplified; cockpit, heat effects and full FlightGear systems are not reproduced. The upstream notes
list known geometry limitations, including excess cabin windows and imperfect
gear doors. No photos or upstream executable code are included.
