Adaptations for this educational viewer:
- Original exterior fuselage; LOD01 wings/engines and nose gear; original wing/body gear components.
- Simulator-specific material extensions replaced with portable glTF PBR materials.
- Original BC-compressed KTX2 diffuse/normal textures decoded, resized to at most 1024 pixels, and encoded as WebP.
- Normal-map blue reconstructed from BC5 RG channels; DirectX Y converted to OpenGL convention.
- Geometry deduplicated, quantised and meshopt-compressed into seven GLB files.
- Original mesh hierarchy, skinning and supported animation channels retained.
- ASOBO BLEND1 scalar skin weights expanded to standard VEC4 before optimisation.
- Positive simulator skin-joint indices remapped from one-based to zero-based; weighted zero uses a fixed identity joint as a compatibility assumption not confirmed by an official optimized-format specification.
- Simulator-invisible helper meshes excluded by the viewer; real tail surfaces are retained.
- Two horizontal-stabilizer skins receive their source Bone rest-frame bind-shape transform in inverse bind matrices, before quantisation; original animation remains live. Source hierarchy and palette assertions guard this specific conversion.
- Source animation clips mapped to simplified child-friendly UI controls. Some aircraft-system sequencing is intentionally omitted.
- Aircraft aligned to the viewer's metre-scale display coordinate system.

Adaptation tooling and application: Pi coding assistant.
Reproduction scripts: scripts/acquire-fbw.py and scripts/optimize-models.mjs.
